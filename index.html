04 / WORKING METHOD

Rigour before
reassurance.

A dashboard is only as useful as the data, assumptions and reasoning behind it. I aim to make those foundations visible.

01

Start with the decision

Clarify the user, the operational question and what a useful answer would change.

02

Interrogate the data

Check completeness, consistency, definitions and the limits of the available records.

03

Choose defensible methods

Use analysis or models suited to the question, then test assumptions and failure modes.

04

Make the result usable

Communicate the evidence, uncertainty and next steps through clear tools and reporting.
