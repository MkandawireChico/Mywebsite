HOW I WORK

From a question
to a useful answer.

Good analysis is more than a model or a chart. It starts with understanding the decision, checking the data, choosing methods that fit the question and communicating the limitations clearly.

01

Frame the problem

Define the operational question, users and decisions the work should support.

02

Build trustworthy data

Validate inputs, document assumptions and make transformations reproducible.

03

Analyse and evaluate

Use appropriate statistical or machine-learning methods and test their limitations.

04

Communicate for action

Translate findings into dashboards, reports and transparent next steps.
