CASE STUDY 05 · PUBLIC HEALTH SUPPLY

Malawi Medicine Stock-Out Analytics

Turning inventory ledgers into auditable stock coverage and replenishment review signals.

View source repository ↗

THE QUESTION

Which facility–medicine combinations show low stock coverage, which records need data-quality review, and how can lead time and safety stock inform reorder guidance?

An inventory analytics workflow combining reproducible daily ledger generation, accounting validation, stock indicators, scenario files, a baseline classifier, SQL examples, Streamlit and Power BI outputs.

01 / CONTEXT

Why stock records matter.

Medicine availability depends on accurate stock movement records. Opening stock, receipts, issues, adjustments and losses should reconcile to closing stock. This project demonstrates how to validate that ledger and derive inventory measures that can be inspected by facility and medicine.

02 / APPROACH

How the inventory analysis works.

01

Generate and validate the ledger

A daily inventory ledger is checked for required columns, duplicate keys, invalid dates, negative balances and accounting discrepancies.

02

Calculate inventory indicators

The workflow derives days of stock, reorder point, reorder quantity and rule-based stock status. Current app coverage uses a trailing 30-record average of daily issues where available.

03

Compare scenario files

Baseline, delayed-delivery, high-demand and improved-policy scenarios support comparison of inventory and replenishment outcomes.

04

Publish analysis outputs

The repository includes Python modules, a Streamlit review interface, SQL examples, a Power BI report and notebook-era exploratory charts.

WHAT THE ANALYSIS SHOWS

The main result is a facility-by-medicine view of current balance, recent issues, estimated days of stock, reorder guidance and ledger quality. Missing or outdated reports remain marked as unknown rather than being treated as zero stock.

VISUAL EVIDENCE

Dashboard screenshots.

04 / PRACTICAL RELEVANCE

Using the inventory view.

A district supply-chain reviewer can select a facility and medicine, inspect recent stock movement, review days of stock and stock status, and investigate whether the ledger balances. Reorder calculations provide transparent review guidance based on lead time, safety stock and target stock inputs.
