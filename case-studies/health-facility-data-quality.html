CASE STUDY 01 · HEALTH INFORMATION

Health Facility Data Quality Intelligence

Turning routine reporting records into a structured data-quality review workflow.

Open live application ↗View source repository ↗

THE QUESTION

How can a health information or M&E team identify reporting-quality issues, understand where they recur and prioritise follow-up?

A reproducible workflow that assesses completeness, timeliness, consistency across reporting sources and unusual values, then presents facility and programme-level review signals.

01 / CONTEXT

The reporting problem.

Health programmes rely on timely and credible routine information. When paper registers, electronic medical records and DHIS2 contain different values—or reports arrive late—teams need a repeatable way to identify records requiring verification. The project models this workflow for a Rumphi District case study.

02 / APPROACH

How the checks work.

01

Data structure

The case-study dataset contains 960 monthly facility-indicator records: 8 illustrative facilities × 12 months × 5 programme areas × 2 indicators.

02

Quality dimensions

Completeness is calculated from required field presence; timeliness compares submission and deadline dates when available; source consistency uses a documented hybrid threshold; outliers are flagged with a grouped IQR rule.

03

Prioritisation

A transparent facility index combines five normalised quality dimensions to help organise follow-up. It is a prioritisation aid, not a validated performance score.

04

Delivery

A Python assessment pipeline feeds a Streamlit workspace and a Power BI report, with facility filters, issue register, source reconciliation and CSV outputs.

WHAT THE ANALYSIS SHOWS

Using these rules, 271 of 960 records received at least one quality flag. Issue counts overlap: 28 missing reported values, 184 late reports, 64 source reviews and 14 IQR outliers. Source consistency was 93.3%. Kachulu had the highest rate-normalised facility risk index (8.1/100).

04 / PRACTICAL RELEVANCE

Using the review workspace.

An M&E officer can filter to a facility or programme, inspect the affected reporting periods, review the source values, assign a follow-up owner and export the issue register. The workflow makes the next verification step visible instead of presenting a single unexplained score.

05 / TECHNOLOGY

Technology used.

Python · Pandas · NumPy · Streamlit · Power BI · Jupyter Notebook

SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

Limitations and interpretation.

This project demonstrates how routine health reporting data can be assessed for completeness, consistency and timeliness. The findings are illustrative and would require validation against actual facility reporting data.
